# CONCEPT: Orbit Breaker (working title)

## The problem and who has it
Casual arcade fans who like brick-breaker games but want something more visual and rewarding than a flat grid. They have a few minutes at a time, usually on a computer, and want a clear goal and a steady difficulty curve.

## Value proposition
A futuristic space brick-breaker with 50 levels, from easy to very hard. The player moves a plate with the mouse to keep a ball in play, smashes geometric blocks (squares, rectangles, circles, triangles) in a galaxy setting, and unlocks extra mechanics as the levels get harder.

## Core loop
1. Move the plate with the mouse.
2. Bounce the ball so it hits blocks.
3. Clear all destructible blocks to finish the level.
4. Lose a life when the ball falls past the plate. Lose the game when lives run out.

## Market
Brick-breaker and Arkanoid-style games have a long-running audience on browsers. The market is crowded, so the differentiator is the space theme, the geometric variety, and a clear 50-level progression. The Day 2 research should confirm whether that angle is worth more than the basic version.
