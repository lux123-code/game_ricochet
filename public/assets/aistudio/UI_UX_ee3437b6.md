# UI_UX

## Screens and user journey
Title screen → Level select (1 to 50, locked/unlocked) → Playing → Level clear / Game over → back to Level select.

- Title: game name, "Play" and "Levels" buttons.
- Level select: grid of 50 numbered tiles. Locked tiles are dimmed. Best score shown on each.
- Playing: play area with HUD on the right (or top): level number, score, lives.
- Pause: press Esc or P. Overlay with Resume and Quit.
- Level clear: message, score, "Next level" button.
- Game over: final score, "Retry" and "Level select" buttons.

## Look and feel
- Background: deep space gradient, slow-moving stars, a distant planet or nebula.
- Palette: dark navy base, cyan and violet accents, warm orange for the ball and sparks.
- Blocks: glowing outlines with a subtle inner gradient. Each shape keeps a distinct color family.
- Plate: metallic with a glowing edge, like a spacecraft thruster.
- Ball: bright core with a short trail.
- Typography: a clean geometric sans-serif for the HUD, a techno display font for titles (with fallbacks).
- Effects: small particle burst when a block breaks. Keep effects light so the game runs smoothly.

## Controls
- Mouse move: moves the plate.
- Left click: launch the ball (if it is on the plate).
- Esc or P: pause.

## Accessibility and comfort
- High-contrast ball and plate against the background.
- Optional reduced-effects toggle in the pause menu.
